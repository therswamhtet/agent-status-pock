# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors must communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback, and
focus discussion on the project and its users. Harassment, discriminatory
language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
or harmful.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
